# Papers to add later

Sample papers found in the October 2026 sweep that are not in the zoo yet, with what is missing.
Remove a line when the paper is added (data/papers/<key>/) or when it turns out not to fit.

- Casey et al. 2026, arXiv:2606.26098: ~1300 LRD-like quasars in SDSS (244 V-shaped). Full table is a journal machine-readable file. `facility: SDSS`.
